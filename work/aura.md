## Product position

Aura sits between three familiar alternatives: an investment bank's wealth
platform, a centralised crypto exchange and a specialist private-wealth
provider. Each solves part of the journey; Aura brings the experience
together around one portfolio.

The trading experience serves two audiences: clients who want a simple,
single-quote Convert flow and clients who need more control through limit,
stop-market, stop-limit and recurring orders.

Across the order types, the product keeps the trading pair, available balance,
estimated result, fees and order status legible. The design offers a simple
entry point while allowing experienced clients to express more precise
trading intent.

Earn lets clients discover a yield product, review its terms, subscribe and
track the resulting position in their portfolio.

### Fixed Earn

Fixed Earn is presented similarly to a term deposit: the product offers a
fixed APY for a defined term, such as 60 or 90 days. Before subscribing, the
client can review the asset, rate, term, minimum and maximum amount, and the
conditions for receiving principal and earned yield at maturity.

Early redemption may be available for selected products. When it is offered,
the relevant exit conditions are shown to the client before confirmation.

### Variable and Strategy Earn

Variable and Strategy products provide market-linked yield. The displayed
rate or estimated range can change with market performance, and product terms
may be flexible or fixed depending on the individual offering.

| Dimension | Fixed Earn | Variable / Strategy Earn |
|---|---|---|
| Return | Fixed APY for the product term | Market-linked rate or estimated range |
| Term | Defined, such as 60 or 90 days | Product-dependent; often flexible |
| Client focus | Certainty of rate and maturity | Potential return with changing market conditions |
| Exit | At maturity or under stated early-redemption terms | Based on the individual product terms |

The main product challenge was clarity: customers need to understand which
values are promised, which are estimates, when funds are available and what
conditions apply before they subscribe.

## Platform evolution

Aura launched in four months on a white-label core to reach market quickly. As the business grew, it needed more flexibility and faster delivery than the third-party core allowed, so we rebuilt the platform in-house while the live product kept running.

<div class="delivery-grid">
  <article><h3>Launch</h3><p>4 months to go live on a white-label core</p></article>
  <article><h3>Live operations</h3><p>7 months in production</p></article>
  <article><h3>Parallel rebuild</h3><p>1 month of planning and 3 months of development, alongside live operations</p></article>
  <article><h3>Client migration</h3><p>live clients moved to the in-house platform smoothly</p></article>
</div>

For clients, the priority was continuity. Their portfolio information and active products carried over, and they only needed to set a new password and 2FA on the new platform.

## Outcomes and lessons

Aura brought custody, funding, trading and yield into one experience across mobile and web, and moved live clients from a white-label core to an in-house platform without disrupting their access to assets or products.

Three principles shaped the work:

1. **Make complexity progressive.** Convert and Fixed Earn are simple entry points; advanced orders and market-linked products are there when clients want more control.
2. **Keep financial information explainable.** Rates, terms, balances and statuses appear at the moment they affect a decision. For example, Earn shows the subscription cutoff time and when a redemption will settle before the client confirms.
3. **Design trust into money movement.** Clear instructions, confirmation details and trackable status matter more than visual novelty. For example, deposits show exact bank details and a status the client can follow until funds arrive.
