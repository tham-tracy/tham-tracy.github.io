<section class="portfolio-section" id="principles">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">How I work</p>
    <h2>Product principles</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-grid two-up">
    <article><h3>Validate the problem before the solution</h3><p>In finance, a wrong product decision costs users money and trust. I look for evidence that a problem is real and worth solving before committing a team to it.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Make complexity progressive</h3><p>Simple entry points alongside advanced capabilities: Convert with conditional orders, fixed yield with market-linked products.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Keep financial information explainable</h3><p>Users should understand both the result and the reason behind it - a purchasing-power number, a redemption date, a fee.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Service teams are users</h3><p>Search, triage and loan-detail views are designed as product experiences for the people supporting customers.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
