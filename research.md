<section class="portfolio-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">How I research</p>
  <h1>Find the right problem<br><span>before building.</span></h1>
  <p class="lede">In finance, a wrong product decision costs users money and trust. So I validate the problem before the solution - with the customers who feel it and the teams who see it every day.</p>
</section>

<section class="portfolio-section" id="method">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">The method at a glance</p>
    <h2>Five steps, from signal to validated decision</h2>
  </div>
  <ol class="method-strip">
    <li><a href="#signal"><span>01</span>Signal</a></li>
    <li><a href="#research"><span>02</span>Research</a></li>
    <li><a href="#insight"><span>03</span>Insight</a></li>
    <li><a href="#decision"><span>04</span>Decision</a></li>
    <li><a href="#validate"><span>05</span>Validate</a></li>
  </ol>

  <div class="method-steps">
    <article class="method-step" id="signal">
      <p class="eyebrow">01 · Signal</p>
      <h3>Notice that something is wrong - or newly possible</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>What it means</dt><dd>A pattern worth investigating: support tickets, slow operations, churn, a market shift, or a channel customers are moving to.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Methods</dt><dd>Support and ops conversations, product analytics, sales and relationship-manager feedback, market and competitor scans.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Pitfalls</dt><dd>Treating one loud request as a trend; starting from a solution someone has already chosen.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="step-callout"><span class="tag">TCBS</span> Higher market activity exposed slow product changes, limited operational visibility and heavy dependence on a closed vendor system.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="method-step" id="research">
      <p class="eyebrow">02 · Research</p>
      <h3>Go to the people and the data</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>What it means</dt><dd>Understand the job users are trying to get done, and what it costs them today.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Methods</dt><dd>Interviews with customers and service teams, shadowing real workflows, analysing the existing product and its data, reviewing competitor journeys.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Pitfalls</dt><dd>Only talking to end customers when internal teams carry most of the pain; asking people what they want instead of what they do.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="step-callout"><span class="tag">TCBS</span> I analysed the existing product, mapped the margin domain into capabilities teams could own independently, and sat with service teams to collect their feedback.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="method-step" id="insight">
      <p class="eyebrow">03 · Insight</p>
      <h3>Name the underlying problem</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>What it means</dt><dd>A short, testable statement of what is really going on - not a list of requested features.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Methods</dt><dd>Affinity mapping, journey maps, separating the customer's question from the system's answer.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Pitfalls</dt><dd>Stopping at the symptom ("the screen is confusing") instead of the cause ("promised and estimated values look the same").</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="step-callout"><span class="tag">Aura</span> Clients need to know which values are promised, which are estimates, when funds are available and what conditions apply - before they subscribe.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="method-step" id="decision">
      <p class="eyebrow">04 · Decision</p>
      <h3>Turn the insight into a product choice</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>What it means</dt><dd>Decide what to build, what not to build, and what success looks like.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Methods</dt><dd>Option framing, trade-off tables, prioritising by customer impact and risk, agreeing success metrics up front.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Pitfalls</dt><dd>Committing to scope before the riskiest assumption is tested.</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="step-callout"><span class="tag">TCBS</span> Separate product policy from each customer's loan state, so business teams manage rules while service teams can still explain every loan.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="method-step" id="validate">
      <p class="eyebrow">05 · Validate</p>
      <h3>Check it worked - and keep listening</h3>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>What it means</dt><dd>Prove the decision solved the problem, then feed what you learn back into the next signal.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Methods</dt><dd>Prototype tests, staged rollouts, adoption and support-volume metrics, follow-up interviews.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Pitfalls</dt><dd>Measuring output (features shipped) instead of outcome (problems solved).</dd></div>
      </dl>
      <p class="step-callout"><span class="tag">TrueProfit</span> We launched the TikTok Shop app in one and a half months and tracked adoption: 200 customers within two months.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="portfolio-section" id="stories">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Research stories</p>
    <h2>The method in real products</h2>
  </div>

  <article class="story-card" id="trueprofit-tiktok">
    <p class="eyebrow">FireGroup · TrueProfit</p>
    <h3>Bringing net-profit reporting to TikTok Shop sellers</h3>
    <dl class="story-steps">
      <div><dt>Signal</dt><dd>TrueProfit helped Shopify store owners see their real net profit, and some of those sellers were expanding their business to TikTok Shop.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Research</dt><dd>I interviewed current TrueProfit customers who were expanding from Shopify to TikTok Shop. I also reached out to TikTok Shop sellers we knew in the market, offering free use of the product in return for an interview.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Insight</dt><dd>TikTok Shop's app marketplace was new, and a TikTok Shop seller's business model can differ from a Shopify seller's, so we couldn't simply copy the Shopify app.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Decision</dt><dd>Build a TrueProfit app for TikTok Shop from scratch. The first version focused on Shopify sellers expanding to TikTok Shop, or shifting their business there to do dropshipping.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Result</dt><dd>Launched in one and a half months, and reached 200 customers within two months.</dd></div>
    </dl>
  </article>

  <article class="story-card" id="trueprofit-ai">
    <p class="eyebrow">FireGroup · TrueProfit</p>
    <h3>Building an AI chatbot for profit questions</h3>
    <dl class="story-steps">
      <div><dt>Signal</dt><dd>Getting a quick answer meant going to the reporting section and reading a report, and new sellers also had to work through set-up.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Research</dt><dd>We gathered what sellers actually ask: our customer-success chat history with users since launch, plus the use cases we expected sellers to bring to the app.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Insight</dt><dd>Sellers wanted to grab their numbers quickly, without navigating the reporting section to find them.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Decision</dt><dd>Build an interactive chatbot that helps sellers with set-up and answers reporting questions directly. A seller can ask "What is my profit trend over the week?" and get the report in the chat, instead of going to the reporting section. We built it as a RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) model over our app knowledge and customer-success chat history.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Result</dt><dd>We wrote about 100 use-case chats and tested 30 of them, with a 90% accuracy bar before release. We then A/B tested, launched to active users and kept improving it from their feedback.</dd></div>
    </dl>
  </article>

  <article class="story-card" id="aura">
    <p class="eyebrow">Hex Trust · Aura</p>
    <h3>Yield products private-wealth clients can understand and trust</h3>
    <dl class="story-steps">
      <div><dt>Signal</dt><dd>Aura showed Fixed and Variable Earn products side by side, each with a different yield. Even when the variable product offered a quite competitive yield, clients still chose fixed.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Research</dt><dd>I researched how other platforms present earn products, from straightforward ones to very complicated ones. Relationship managers sat with us every week to share how introducing yield products felt and how clients talked about them.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Insight</dt><dd>Clients need to know which values are promised, which are estimates, when funds are available and what conditions apply before they subscribe.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Decision</dt><dd>Present Fixed Earn like a term deposit - fixed APY, defined term, clear maturity conditions - and keep market-linked Variable and Strategy products visibly separate, with exit terms shown before confirmation.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Result</dt><dd>Earn is live in Aura: before confirming, clients see the subscription cutoff time and when a redemption will settle.</dd></div>
    </dl>

  </article>

  <article class="story-card" id="tcbs">
    <p class="eyebrow">TCBS · Techcom Securities</p>
    <h3>Service teams are users too</h3>
    <dl class="story-steps">
      <div><dt>Signal</dt><dd>Higher market activity exposed slow product changes and limited operational visibility in the margin lending system.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Research</dt><dd>Analysed the existing product, then sat and worked directly with service teams to collect their feedback on how they found loans, worked out what customers owed and took the next action. We then aligned with them on what to deliver.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Insight</dt><dd>Service teams were piecing information together across systems to answer a single customer question - so customers got slow, unclear answers.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Decision</dt><dd>Design loan search, triage and detail views as a product: status first, paid vs. remaining, and traceable actions in one place.</dd></div>
      <div><dt>Result</dt><dd>Part of a seven-month redesign that migrated approximately 900,000 customers across seven teams, without rollback.</dd></div>
    </dl>

      <h3>SMB merchants</h3>
      <p class="customer-where">TrueProfit</p>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>They care about</dt><dd>Knowing their real profit after fees, ads and costs - quickly, without spreadsheets.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Good ways to research them</dt><dd>Short calls and in-app feedback; they are busy owners, so respect their time and look at their actual data.</dd></div>

    <article>
      <h3>Private-wealth clients</h3>
      <p class="customer-where">Aura</p>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>They care about</dt><dd>Safety of assets, clarity of terms and a service experience that matches their bank.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Good ways to research them</dt><dd>Working through relationship managers, and testing how clearly terms, rates and settlement times read.</dd></div>
      </dl>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Retail investors &amp; internal ops</h3>
      <p class="customer-where">TCBS</p>
      <dl>
        <div><dt>They care about</dt><dd>Investors: purchasing power and loan status they can trust. Ops: finding the right case and acting on it fast.</dd></div>
        <div><dt>Good ways to research them</dt><dd>Sitting with service teams, tracing real cases end to end, and treating internal tools as product.</dd></div>
      </dl>
